NASA selected Relativity Space to build and launch Aeolus, a 2028 Mars orbiter designed to collect atmospheric measurements. The spacecraft will carry four instruments to gather data on dust, winds, and temperatures, following NASA's model of combining government science with commercial infrastructure.
