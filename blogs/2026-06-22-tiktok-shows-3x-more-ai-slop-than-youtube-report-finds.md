Kapwing's analysis of more than 10,000 TikTok videos found 59% are AI-generated, compared to YouTube's 21%. Children's content showed the highest rates at 57%, with #cartoonkids reaching 97%; TikTok had labeled 1.3 billion AI videos by November.
