UC Davis researchers demonstrated a brain-computer interface enabling an ALS patient to communicate with 99% accuracy in controlled settings and roughly 92% in daily use. Casey Harrell has operated the system independently since 2023, accumulating over 3,800 usage hours while maintaining full-time employment.
