A Chinese Zhuque-2E rocket's upper stage disintegrated after a June 9 launch, creating 100-150 debris fragments in low-Earth orbit. While atmospheric drag should remove most debris within months, Chinese rocket bodies in long-lived orbits increased 150% over five years.
