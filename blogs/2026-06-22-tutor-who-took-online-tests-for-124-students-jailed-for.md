Shahid Adnan, a 43-year-old Liverpool tutor, received a three-year sentence for completing assignments and exams for more than 120 students, earning 300,000 pounds. Authorities found credential spreadsheets and evidence of network access across multiple universities, with bank accounts holding over 2 million pounds.
