SpaceX agreed to acquire AI coding assistant startup Cursor for $60 billion in stock. Founded in 2022, Cursor reached $1 billion in annualized revenue and ranked 37th on CNBC's 2026 Disruptor 50 list; the deal follows SpaceX's earlier merger with xAI.
