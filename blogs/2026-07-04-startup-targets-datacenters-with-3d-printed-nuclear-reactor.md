Ampera has unveiled a 3D-printed nuclear reactor with a silicon-carbide core designed for thorium-based microreactors, expecting a power generation system by 2027 and a full nuclear module by 2030. The technology targets delivering 15-30 megawatts to datacenters without refueling for up to 30 years.
