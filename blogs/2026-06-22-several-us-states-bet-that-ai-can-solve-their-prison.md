Nineteen prison systems are partnering with nonprofit Recidiviz to consolidate fragmented prisoner records into unified digital dashboards giving corrections staff access to court records and parole hearing notes. Recidiviz reports a 16% recidivism reduction, though broader trend data remains incomplete.
