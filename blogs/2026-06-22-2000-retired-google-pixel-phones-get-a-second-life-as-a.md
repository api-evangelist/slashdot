UC San Diego researchers partnered with Google to build a private cloud from 2,000 retired Pixel Fold motherboards, with 25-50 phones delivering server-equivalent performance. The chips retain enough performance for various tasks, with Android replaced by Linux, Kubernetes orchestration, and custom PCBs for power and networking.
