Claude Guillemot, 69, who co-founded Ubisoft with his four brothers in 1986, died in a plane crash in La Baule, France. He oversaw franchises including Assassin's Creed and Far Cry and chaired Guillemot Corp., which makes gaming accessories; his brother Yves continues as CEO.
